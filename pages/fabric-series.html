Marble is naturally durable but requires proper sealing and care to maintain its beauty over time. It can withstand daily use in residential settings when maintained properly.

Use a soft cloth or sponge with warm water and mild pH-neutral soap. Avoid acidic cleaners like vinegar or lemon juice which can etch the surface.
